## Quick Fact Checks

**Q: Does current service.go have a message pump?**  
A: No. Billing channel is never read.

**Q: Is Start() race-condition safe?**  
A: No. Returns before runManager() is guaranteed to be running.

**Q: What happens to messages on connection loss?**  
A: They're unsettled and either accumulate or redelivered by broker.

**Q: Is context deadline respected after Start()?**  
A: No. runCtx = context.Background() ignores caller's deadline.

**Q: Are there goroutine leaks?**  
A: Yes. time.After() creates orphaned timers on context cancellation.
